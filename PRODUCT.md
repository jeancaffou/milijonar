# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Viewers, researchers, former contestants, and prospective contestants use the catalogue to find authoritative programme records, follow complete contestant runs, compare seasons, and study the kinds of questions that recur. They need dense information to remain easy to scan and every claim to lead back to its programme context.

## Product Purpose

Provide a reliable, self-contained, bilingual catalogue of the Slovenian television quiz *Milijonar*. Success means that seasons, episodes, contestants, complete runs, questions, answers, dates, winnings, statistics, and archive images can be found quickly and understood without access to the source media. Interface text is bilingual; programme questions and answer options remain in original Slovenian.

## Brand Personality

Precise, archival, professional. The interface should feel recognisably connected to the programme without imitating broadcast graphics so literally that information becomes harder to use.

## Anti-references

No promotional slogans, sentimental taglines, vague claims, machine-translated game content, novelty copy, arbitrary decorative imagery, dashboard clichés, or terminology that sounds unnatural in Slovenian. Do not expose internal media filenames, timestamps, processing notes, or research jargon in public pages.

## Design Principles

1. Make the record easy to verify and easier to navigate.
2. Preserve original programme language and context.
3. Prefer direct labels, ranked information, and stable links over decorative copy.
4. Keep dense archival material legible on desktop and mobile.
5. Distinguish measured facts from interpretation and methodology.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, keyboard-operable navigation and disclosures, visible focus states, text alternatives, sufficient contrast, responsive layouts without horizontal page overflow, and language metadata for Slovenian programme content shown inside English pages. Motion must respect reduced-motion preferences and never carry essential meaning.
