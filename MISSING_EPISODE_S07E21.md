# S07E21 / S06E57 Broadcast Audit

Research completed: 2026-07-13

## Conclusion

There is no missing unique S07E21 programme.

Planet broadcast 20 unique S07 premieres: 15 from 29 January through
28 February 2024 and five from 10 May through 7 June 2024. The Milijonar slot
on Friday, 14 June 2024 showed the 15 December 2023 S06E57 programme again,
with Andreja Remec, Gregor Bilban, and Žan Papič. The public 21-programme count
includes that rerun as the sixth May/June broadcast; it is not a count of 21
unique produced episodes.

This conclusion does not depend on assuming that the local file was captured
from the intended slot. Planet's own article index independently promotes Žan
for 14 June, and Planet's June schedule places Milijonar in the Friday prime
slot that evening. The local recording is used only after that date is
established, to identify the complete programme and results that were shown.

## Independent Web Chronology

### Original Žan Papič appearance: 15 December 2023

The original [Siol/Planet article](https://siol.net/planet-tv/milijonar/odziv-zana-papica-ko-so-ga-poklicali-za-nastop-v-milijonarju-vas-bo-nasmejal-video-622440)
is dated Friday, 15 December 2023 at 13:02. It says Žan Papič would appear in
Milijonar that night at 20:00 and play for Botrstvo v Sloveniji. This dates the
produced programme independently of every local filename.

### Late S07 sequence shown by Planet

Planet's [dated Milijonar article index](https://www.planet-tv.si/clanek/?page=1&showtype=milijonar)
shows this order:

| Index date | Planet item | Programme identity |
|---|---|---|
| 24 May 2024 | `Godler nasmejal: Ste me slučajno videli priti z balonom na snemanje?` | Names Štefan from Gačnik pri Pesnici and his 2022 balloon-championship question; this exact game is in local S07E18 |
| 31 May 2024 | `Bo tekmovalec to odločitev obžaloval?` | Names Albin from Ribnica and his primary-school education-level Q4; this exact game is in local S07E19 |
| 14 June 2024 | `Odziv Žana Papiča ...` | Says Žan will be in Milijonar `nocoj ob 20. uri`; this is the already documented S06E57 charity game |

The [Albin article](https://www.planet-tv.si/clanek/bo-tekmovalec-to-odlocitev-obzaloval/)
also carries a 31 May timestamp and calls that night's programme a new episode.
The [archived Planet Milijonar page from 22 June 2024](https://web.archive.org/web/20240622021520id_/https://www.planet-tv.si/oddaje/milijonar/)
places the newly refreshed Žan item first, followed by Albin and Štefan. Its
Žan thumbnail filename contains `2024-06-12`, consistent with a new promotion
prepared immediately before the 14 June broadcast.

Planet's [June 2024 programme schedule](https://www.planet-tv.si/media/jl3a3i3c/cenik-planet-junij-2024.pdf)
shows Milijonar in the Friday prime slot on both 7 June and 14 June. On 14 June
there is one Friday prime Milijonar slot, followed by `Ta teden z Juretom
Godlerjem`; the Žan article explicitly advertises him for that night's 20:00
showing. This establishes when the duplicate programme was featured even if a
local stream capture or filename were wrong.

### Why the Žan page's `Objavljeno 24. 05. 2024` is not an air date

The current [Planet Žan page](https://www.planet-tv.si/clanek/odziv-zana-papica-ko-so-ga-povabili-v-milijonarja-vas-bo-nasmejal/)
shows `Objavljeno 24. 05. 2024`, `Posodobljeno 15. 06. 2024`, while Planet's
article index dates the item to 14 June. The 24 May field is a CMS migration or
import artefact, not evidence that Žan aired on 24 May.

There is a direct control example. Planet's migrated
[`Milijonar odslej ... ob petkih`](https://www.planet-tv.si/clanek/milijonar-odslej-na-planetu-ob-petkih-ob-20-uri/)
page also says `Objavljeno 24. 05. 2024`, but its own text advertises Katja
Koren for Friday, 31 March. The [original Siol copy](https://siol.net/planet-tv/milijonar/milijonar-odslej-na-planetu-ob-petkih-ob-20-uri-602914)
is correctly dated 30 March 2023. Several other obviously older imported
Planet articles carry the same 24 May 2024 stamp.

Fandom appears to have treated the migrated Žan page date as an episode date.
That likely caused its late-season entries to shift: Žan at 24 May, Štefan at
31 May, Albin at 7 June, and a blank 14 June row. Planet's own dated index and
the exact question content contradict that order.

## The 7 June Programme

No contestant-named Planet article was found for 7 June. The mapping therefore
uses the following combined evidence and is stated with that limitation:

- Planet's schedule has a Milijonar premiere-time slot on Friday, 7 June.
- Planet's dated content fixes local S07E19/Albin to 31 May.
- The next complete local programme in source order is S07E20, with Brigita
  Rozina and Evelina Kos.
- Direct frame inspection shows `planet PREMIERA` on S07E18, S07E19, and
  S07E20. The archived Žan capture has only the plain `planet` bug.
- The next dated Planet promotion, for 14 June, is the old Žan programme.

Accordingly, the only remaining complete S07 premiere maps to 7 June:

| Canonical episode | Air date | Contestants and final prizes |
|---|---|---|
| S07E18 | 24 May 2024 | Peter Remec: EUR 2,500; Štefan Vrečič: EUR 750 |
| S07E19 | 31 May 2024 | Albin Ponikvar: EUR 0; Timotej Rupar: EUR 300; Gal Jeršin: EUR 1,000 |
| S07E20 | 7 June 2024 | Brigita Rozina: EUR 1,000; Evelina Kos: EUR 2,500 |

The names and prizes in this table come from the visually verified local game
evidence. The web evidence establishes the dates and broadcast sequence.

## Why Wikipedia And Fandom Show 21

The [Slovenian Wikipedia season table](https://sl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milijonar)
splits S07 into 15 January/February shows and six Friday shows from 10 May
through 14 June. Six scheduled Friday broadcasts did occur, but the sixth was
the S06E57 rerun. Therefore `15 + 6 = 21` is a broadcast-slot count, not a
unique-episode count. For a catalogue of unique game content the correct count
is `15 + 5 = 20`.

The current [Fandom Season 19 page](https://millionaire.fandom.com/wiki/Season_19_%28Slovenia%29)
is internally inconsistent: its infobox says 16 episodes while its body has 21
date headings. It assigns Žan to 24 May, Štefan to 31 May, Albin to 7 June, and
leaves 14 June empty. Planet's article index directly disproves those
assignments. Fandom is not evidence of a separate Albin episode or any new
14 June roster.

## Local Programme Identity

After the web chronology establishes 14 June as the Žan showing, direct visual
comparison identifies what Planet reran:

- same Andreja Remec opening and fast-fingers round;
- same Andreja run and Q12 walk-away;
- same Gregor Bilban fast-fingers round, run, and Q10 walk-away;
- same Žan Papič charity introduction, questions, and Q13 walk-away;
- same studio audience, answer states, reactions, and final amounts;
- all 35 question boards in the same contestant/question order.

Thirty catalogue boards have text-identical transcriptions. The other five
differ only in transcription details such as accents, capitalization, or
`8½` versus `8 1/2`; the frames show the same boards. The two media files are
not byte-identical and differ slightly in duration, which is consistent with
separate broadcast captures. The original S06 capture carries `PREMIERA`; the
June capture does not.

No independent source names an additional 14 June contestant, and the official
promotion instead names Žan. There is therefore no missing roster or prize set
to reconstruct.

## Catalogue Disposition

- Keep canonical S07 at E01-E20, ending 7 June 2024.
- Keep S06E57 as the sole canonical representation of Andreja Remec, Gregor
  Bilban, and Žan Papič.
- Keep the 35 duplicate question rows and three duplicate contestant rows out
  of the active CSVs.
- Keep the later media/subtitle source under
  `duplicate_sources/Milijonar.S07E21.rebroadcast-of-S06E57.1080p.x265.*`.
- Keep its 71 evidence images under
  `duplicate_sources/evidence/s07e21_rebroadcast_of_s06e57_frames/` as
  provenance, not active catalogue evidence.

## Confidence And Remaining Limitation

The 14 June rerun identification is high confidence: the original 2023 date,
Planet's 14 June article-index promotion, Planet's 14 June schedule, and the
complete visual programme match all agree.

The S07E20-to-7-June mapping is also strong, but no indexed web article naming
Brigita Rozina or Evelina Kos on that date was found. It rests on Planet's
dated adjacent content, the official 7 June slot, source order, and the direct
`PREMIERA` frame. That limitation does not create a plausible missing S07E21:
Planet independently advertised the old Žan programme for the 14 June prime
slot.
